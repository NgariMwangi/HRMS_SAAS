# Performance reviews

HR creates a review with a performance period, goal-entry roles (HR, assigned manager/supervisor, employee, or a combination), and a separate self-review start/end date. Assign employees and start goal entry. Approved goals must total 100% before starting goal tracking. Employees record progress separately from entering goal definitions.

HR opens self-review within its scheduled dates. Employees submit their self-reviews; then HR starts manager assessment, followed by discussion, preparation of final results, and closure. Optional calibration remains available. Employees acknowledge results and HR finalizes individual records before closing the review.

Reviews display Upcoming, In progress, or Completed and a next task. Existing records, scoring, permissions, and audit history are preserved. The existing database tables and legacy cycle API remain compatible; users use Reviews rather than cycle stage controls.
