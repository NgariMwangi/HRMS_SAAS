Basic salary for new starters
============================

Payroll uses the employee's joining date. If they join after the period starts,
their basic pay is calculated as inclusive calendar days from joining to period
end, divided by 30, multiplied by their saved monthly basic salary. Days are
capped at 30. A person employed throughout the period receives their full salary,
including February and months with 31 days. With no joining date, full salary is
used. This calculation does not count attendance records or unpaid leave.

For example, joining April 16 gives 15 payable days: a monthly basic of KES 30,000
becomes KES 15,000. Joining March 16 gives 16 days. Allowances and separately
scheduled benefits and deductions retain their existing rules. For a gross-only
salary, the saved gross salary is the monthly salary being prorated.

Employees joining after the period ends are shown as ineligible in payroll review
and excluded from the run. Taxes and contributions use the adjusted earnings.
The payslip salary line shows the day fraction in both the portal and PDF.

Saved compensation remains the full monthly rate. Draft reruns recalculate from
the joining date; approved runs retain their existing snapshots. Existing runs
are not changed automatically. No database migration is required.
